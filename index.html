A Letter For You

Ek chhota sa letter, dil ki bahut saari feelings.

Meri pyaari Rinku ❤️ Pata hai, tumhari tareef karne ke liye words kam pad jaate hain. Tumhari aankhon ki baat alag hai, tumhari smile ki baat alag hai, aur tumse baat karne mein jo sukoon milta hai, woh sabse alag hai. Main chahta hoon ki tum hamesha apni khubsurati par nahi, apne dil aur apni achhai par bhi utna hi proud feel karo. Tumhe hamesha respect, care aur pyaar mile — aur meri taraf se toh jitna ho sake utna zaroor. Kabhi tumhara din achha ho toh main tumhari khushi mein khush hona chahta hoon. Kabhi din mushkil ho toh tumhari baat sunna chahta hoon. Har baat ka solution dena zaroori nahi, kabhi bas saath rehna bhi pyaar hota hai. Tum mere liye sirf ek beautiful face nahi ho, Rinku. Tum woh person ho jiske saath main chhoti-chhoti baaton mein bhi khushi dhoondhna chahta hoon. Thank you for being my favourite person.
Hamesha pyaar ke saath, RJ ♡
